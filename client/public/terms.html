No accounts, nothing to lose

There is no sign-up and no persistent profile. Your callsign is generated per connection and belongs to nobody. Your Aim Rating history lives in your own browser and is yours to clear. Because there is nothing stored, there is nothing that can be lost, restored or transferred.

Fair play

  • attack, flood or attempt to disrupt the service, or connect to it in volume for anything other than playing it;

The game is authoritative on the server precisely so that cheating is uninteresting: your client sends aim and button state, and everything else it draws came back from the server. Connections that abuse the service may be dropped.
